# Influencer Marketing Impact Analysis – Delhi NCR

## Overview
This repository presents an academic and data-driven research study analyzing the impact of influencer marketing on consumer trust, platform effectiveness, and purchase decisions in the Delhi NCR region.

The study is based on primary survey data collected from 150 social media users and applies Python-based statistical techniques to derive meaningful marketing insights.

## Objectives of the Study
- To analyze consumer trust in influencer marketing
- To evaluate the influence of influencers on purchase decisions
- To identify the most effective social media platform for influencer marketing
- To examine reasons behind consumers unfollowing influencers

## Research Methodology
- Research Design: Quantitative, cross-sectional study
- Data Collection: Structured online questionnaire
- Sample Size: 150 respondents (Delhi NCR)
- Data Analysis: Python-based statistical analysis

## Tools & Technologies Used
- Python (Pandas, NumPy, SciPy, StatsModels, Matplotlib)
- Jupyter Notebook
- Microsoft Excel

## Repository Contents
- **Dataset**: Survey dataset used for analysis
- **Python_Analysis**: Jupyter notebooks containing data cleaning and statistical analysis
- **Research_Paper**: Final academic research paper (PDF)
- **Presentation**: Final project presentation (PPT)

## Key Insights
- Consumer trust in influencer marketing is moderate and not campaign-format dependent
- Influencer marketing plays a supportive role in purchase decisions
- Instagram emerges as the most influential platform
- Overly aggressive promotions lead to audience disengagement
